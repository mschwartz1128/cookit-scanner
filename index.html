# myMatSH — Voice Narration Script **For Professional Voice Over | Screen Recording Walkthrough** **Estimated Runtime: ~6–7 minutes at conversational pace** --- ## PRODUCTION NOTES FOR VOICE TALENT - **Tone:** Warm, professional, confident — respectful of the sacred nature of the work, but modern and approachable. Think "trusted colleague," not "infomercial." - **Pace:** Measured and clear. This is a feature walkthrough; viewers need time to follow along visually. - **Pronunciation Guide:** - **myMatSH** — "my-MATCH" (the SH is silent in spelling but pronounced as "match") - **Shidduch / Shidduchim** — "shih-DOOKH" / "shih-doo-KHEEM" (gentle guttural KH) - **Shadchan / Shadchanim** — "SHAHD-khun" / "shahd-khuh-NEEM" - **Hashkafa** — "hahsh-KAH-fah" - **Yeshiva** — "yeh-SHEE-vah" - **Eretz Yisroel** — "EH-retz yis-ROY-el" - **Pauses:** Brief pause at every [PAUSE] marker. Longer pause at section breaks. --- ## SCENE 1 — OPENING / LANDING PAGE > Welcome to **myMatSH** — a modern platform built to streamline the sacred work of making shidduchim. [PAUSE] > > Designed specifically for the Orthodox Jewish community, myMatSH gives shadchanim the tools they need to connect, collaborate, and bring couples together — all in one secure, professional environment. > > From the landing page, users can log in, sign up, or access the dedicated organization portal. Prospective shadchanim can also apply for an account through a simple vetting process. --- ## SCENE 2 — ABOUT & MISSION > Our **About** and **Mission** pages introduce the four core values behind the platform: increased visibility, a single unified profile for every single, efficient matching, and secure collaboration between shadchanim. [PAUSE] > > myMatSH was built on a simple idea — that broader networks, smarter search, and faster, more private connections lead to more successful shidduchim. --- ## SCENE 3 — THE DASHBOARD > Once logged in, the shadchan lands on a personalized dashboard — the central hub of the platform. [PAUSE] > > Four live summary cards give an at-a-glance view of activity: **My Singles**, **Active Suggestions**, **Tasks Due**, and **Unread Messages**. > > A quick-action banner walks users through the three-step workflow at the heart of myMatSH: add singles, create suggestions, and track progress. [PAUSE] > > Below, recent singles and upcoming tasks are surfaced for instant access — so nothing falls through the cracks. --- ## SCENE 4 — SINGLES MANAGEMENT > The **Singles** section is where the real work happens. [PAUSE] > > Shadchanim can view their own singles, browse all singles on the platform, or focus on those who are currently unrepresented. Powerful filters let users search by gender, status, hashkafa, age range, height, private labels, and more. > > Adding a new single is remarkably fast. With our **AI-powered resume upload**, a shadchan can simply upload a PDF — or even snap a photo of a printed resume — and Claude AI will automatically fill in every relevant field. [PAUSE] Built-in duplicate detection ensures the same single is never added twice. > > For those who prefer a full profile, a seven-step guided wizard captures everything: basic information, contact details, physical description, education, family background, and a complete personal profile. [PAUSE] > > Need to move quickly? The **Quick Add** form captures just the essentials — full details can always be completed later. --- ## SCENE 5 — THE SINGLE PROFILE > Each single has a rich, detailed profile page. [PAUSE] > > At the top, action buttons let a shadchan instantly create a match, add a task, manage private labels, or download the full profile as a PDF. > > A unique feature — **"I Know This Single"** — allows shadchanim to mark themselves as familiar with a candidate, building a network of trusted references right inside the profile. > > Tabs organize everything: the full profile, related matches, tasks, dating history, and private notes. [PAUSE] Private photos uploaded by a shadchan remain visible only to that shadchan — protecting privacy at every step. --- ## SCENE 6 — SUGGESTIONS > The **Suggestions** module is where shidduchim come to life. [PAUSE] > > Shadchanim can view suggestions in a clean table format — or switch to a visual Kanban board that organizes every match by stage: Pending, Current, Going Out, On Hold, Past, Engaged, and finally — Married. > > Creating a new suggestion is as simple as selecting a boy, selecting a girl, and adding notes. From there, the platform tracks every step of the journey. --- ## SCENE 7 — CALENDAR & TASKS > Staying on top of follow-ups is critical — and **myMatSH** makes it effortless. [PAUSE] > > A combined tasks panel and full monthly calendar view keeps every deadline in sight. Days with active tasks are highlighted, today's date is always front and center, and adding a new task takes just a click. --- ## SCENE 8 — MESSAGES & GROUPS > The platform's built-in **messaging system** lets shadchanim communicate securely without leaving the app. [PAUSE] > > And with **Groups**, shadchanim can collaborate as a team — sharing access to singles and suggestions within a private group context. Perfect for organizations, family teams, or mentorship pairings. --- ## SCENE 9 — DIRECTORIES > The **Shadchanim Directory** makes it easy to find and connect with other shadchanim — searchable by name, organization, or location, with experience level and style clearly displayed on every card. [PAUSE] > > The **Organizations** page highlights respected shidduch organizations like Adopt A Shadchan and MSI, making affiliation and collaboration simple. --- ## SCENE 10 — LABELS & PRIVACY > **Labels** are private organizational tags — completely invisible to other users. [PAUSE] > > Whether it's "on the ball," "out of town," or any custom category, labels help shadchanim organize their singles the way they think — privately, and only for themselves. --- ## SCENE 11 — MY PROFILE > Every shadchan has their own profile — managed across four simple tabs: personal information, availability and contact preferences, references and settings, and private labels. [PAUSE] > > Shadchanim can specify languages spoken, years of experience, age brackets they work with, preferred contact methods, and even mark themselves as available for advocacy. [PAUSE] Privacy controls let users hide personal details, and notification preferences keep them informed by email or text. --- ## SCENE 12 — INTELLIGENT FEATURES > Throughout the platform, **AI assistance** is built in where it matters most. [PAUSE] > > Resume upload and auto-fill. Camera-based resume capture. Intelligent duplicate detection. One-click PDF export of any profile. And a floating AI help assistant — always available in the bottom corner — ready to answer any platform question in natural language. --- ## SCENE 13 — DESIGNED FOR EVERY DEVICE > Whether on desktop or mobile, **myMatSH** adapts beautifully. [PAUSE] > > A clean left sidebar organizes every feature on desktop. On mobile, a streamlined bottom navigation puts the most-used tools just a tap away. And a guided product tour is always available — making onboarding effortless for new shadchanim. --- ## SCENE 14 — CLOSING > **myMatSH** — bringing modern tools to timeless work. [PAUSE] > > More visibility. Smarter search. Stronger collaboration. And a secure, private home for every shidduch you make. [PAUSE] > > Visit **mymatsh.vercel.app** to learn more — or apply today to join the platform. --- ## ALTERNATE SHORT CLOSE (if needed for time) > **myMatSH** — streamlining the sacred work of making shidduchim. Visit us today. --- **END OF SCRIPT**
